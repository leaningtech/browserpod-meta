A deck is a markdown file. A line of three dashes starts a new slide.

What SLOTH does

Write slides in markdown

Top-level headings render as large ASCII art, code blocks are highlighted and a pause line reveals a slide step by step. Speaker notes stay on your presenter view.

Share a live view by link

Present mode serves an audience page on a shareable link. The page follows your slides as you move through them, as clean HTML slides or with a terminal look.

Run code on a slide

Press r to run a slide's Node.js snippet. Output streams in a pane beside the slide, and an app that opens a port appears next to the terminal.

Collect survey responses

Add single choice, multiple choice, rating and free text questions to a deck. Responses are saved as a CSV file inside your browser tab, ready to download.

Use your phone as a clicker

Scan the QR code on the presenter view and move through your slides from your phone.

Choose from hundreds of themes

SLOTH includes its own themes plus 365 palettes from the Gogh collection, with a picker to fine-tune individual colours.

Runs on your device

SLOTH runs a Node.js process inside your browser tab through BrowserPod. Your decks, files and survey responses stay in the tab until you download them, and the app is ready to use as soon as it loads.
