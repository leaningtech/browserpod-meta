Convert a WebP image to PNG on your own device. Your file stays with you.

How to convert WebP to PNG

  1. Drop a WebP file above, or choose one from your device.
  2. Check that PNG is selected, then press Convert.
  3. Download the PNG file.

What to expect

  • PNG opens in most image editors and viewers, which makes it a safe choice when a program refuses a WebP file.
  • The image keeps its original pixel dimensions.
  • PNG output is lossless and keeps transparency.
  • The conversion runs in your browser tab, so the file stays on your device.
